# Code of Conduct

These tools are built for Triton Nexus Night, a casual Riftbound community at UC San Diego. Everyone who uses, reports issues on, or contributes to this project is expected to help keep it welcoming.

## Our standards

Please:

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers to the game or to coding.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and accept feedback the same way.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Respect people's privacy. Don't post player names, contact details, Discord IDs or ratings beyond what the public standings already show.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes or language of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Trying to change ratings, results or check-ins for anyone in bad faith.

## Scope

This applies to this repository (issues, pull requests, discussions and code) and to anyone representing the project in other places, such as the club Discord, when talking about these tools.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer, [@bcheng-dev](https://github.com/bcheng-dev), on GitHub or reach out to a Triton Nexus Night organizer on Discord. Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is harmful to the community.
